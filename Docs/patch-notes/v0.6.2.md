# The Witch v0.6.2

Balance pass on Hex and potion payoffs, plus two card reworks.

## Reworks
- **Eye of Newt** — Potions now hit one additional random target (upgraded: ALL valid targets). Now creates an Unstable Ember Jar instead of a Noxious Brew.
- **Cloak of Moonlight** — Now costs 1 (was 2). Triggers on Hex instead of card/potion creation: whenever you trigger Hex, gain Block.

## Buffs
- **Call the Pack** — Block 6 → 7. Upgrade now shuffles upgraded Gnash+ into your draw pile (upgrade Block bonus 3 → 2, no longer adds a third Gnash).
- **Creeping Vines** — Brambles 6 → 7.
- **Gather Herbs** — Upgrade now also grants +2 Block.
- **Bottomless Cauldron** — Upgrade now grants Innate (was cost reduction).
- **Broken Pact** — Upgrade now reduces cost by 1 (was +2 Strength per Familiar).
- **Embrace the Wilds** — Upgrade now also softens the draw penalty.
- **Read the Bones** — Upgrade now grants +1 draw next turn (was +1 Energy next turn).
- **Incant** — Upgrade now grants +1 Hex (was +3 Block).

## Nerfs
- **Bottle Barrage** — Damage 7 → 5.
- **Capture Soul** — Cost 0 → 1, Hex 2 → 1.
- **Hexblast** — Hex 3 → 2.
- **Read the Bones** — Hex 3 → 2.
- **Incant** — Hex 2 → 1.
- **Vexing Strike** — Damage 9 → 7 (upgrade +3 → +2).
- **Pact of Agony** — Damage 13 → 10.
- **Torment** — Damage 5 → 3.
- **Volatile Vapors** — Damage per potion 6 → 5.
- **Wax and Wane** — Stacks 2 → 1.
- **Cursed Spellbook** — Energy 3 → 2 (upgrade now restores +1 Energy and +1 card).
- **Polymorph** — Now chooses 1 card (was 2), which becomes 2 Rats; upgrade chooses 2 cards (was cost reduction).
- **Nimble** (Cat token) — Now Energy only, no longer gains Block.
- **Vial of Smoke** — Block 8 → 7.

## Other
- **Hidden in Smoke** — Created Puffs of Smoke are now Unstable.
- Fixed: potion copied by Gather Herbs now correctly becomes Unstable when the original does.
- Polymorph now shows the transform effect for every Rats created.
- Junk potions are now "thrown at" the Merchant, as nature intended.
